Office Attendance Sheet App

Professional Attendance Tracking & Financial Management System
Cloud-Hosted GPS-Verified Zero-Installation Professional Reports

Product Overview

The Office Attendance Sheet App is a modern, cloud-based workforce management solution designed for offices and workshops that require accurate attendance tracking, GPS-verified clock-in/out, and automated financial balance management. Built with enterprise-grade reliability, it eliminates paper-based attendance systems and manual calculations while providing professional reporting capabilities.

Key Features

📍 GPS Location Tracking

Every clock-in/out captures real-time GPS coordinates with reverse geocoding to display human-readable addresses.

🔗 Permanent Login Links

Each employee receives a unique, permanent URL for instant one-click access — no passwords to remember or reset.

💰 Auto Financial Carry-Over

Remaining balances automatically roll over to the next day. Starting balances calculate in real-time as amounts are entered.

📊 Live Dashboard

Admin panel displays real-time statistics: total employees, currently clocked in, attendance records, and office settings.

📄 Professional PDF Reports

Generate individual employee monthly reports or comprehensive company-wide attendance and financial summaries.

📱 Installable (PWA)

Employees can install the app on their phone's home screen like a native app — works across all modern browsers.

• GPS Verification: Prevents false clock-ins by capturing exact location data
• Row-Level Security: Database-level access controls protect employee data
• Zero Maintenance: Fully managed cloud infrastructure — no servers to maintain
• 99.9% Uptime: Enterprise-grade hosting with automatic failover and global CDN

Financial Balance Flow

The app implements an automated daily balance carry-over system:

Day Starting Balance Amount Added Expenses Remaining Balance
Day 1 $0.00 +$5,000.00 -$3,000.00 $2,000.00
Day 2 $2,000.00 +$2,000.00 -$1,500.00 $2,500.00
Day 3 $2,500.00 +$1,000.00 -$800.00 $2,700.00

Formula: Starting Balance + Amount Added - Expenses = Remaining Balance (carries to next day automatically)
